GoodGame messages now show the icon the protocol actually sends — star, cup,
eagle and the rest — plus a GoodGame+ badge for the tier held. Unlike Twitch,
GoodGame publishes no badge API, so the name-to-file mapping was read out of
their own stylesheet and confirmed against live traffic. Roles that have no icon
of their own keep their text chip.
